# Contagion

Diseases in RimWorld don't just appear out of nowhere anymore — they spread.

Contagion turns illness into something that moves through your colony. A single sick visitor, a contaminated meal, or an untended corpse can become an outbreak that jumps from pawn to pawn. How far it gets is up to you.

## What it does

- **Diseases spread.** Person to person (coughs, close contact, shared spaces), through contaminated food and cooking, from handling or butchering infected corpses, and via environmental carriers like mosquitoes and tsetse flies.
- **Outbreaks have an origin.** First-case alerts tell you where a wave came from so you can react — quarantine the sick, mask up your cooks, deal with the bodies.
- **Animals get sick too.** Livestock and wild animals carry their own illnesses, hidden until a vet diagnoses them. An infected herd is its own kind of emergency.
- **Protection matters.** Masks and sealed apparel cut transmission. A bare-handed cook spreads far more than a masked one.
- **You set the pressure.** Difficulty, how aggressively outbreaks are suppressed, whether the storyteller or Contagion itself decides when disease arrives, and how often — all in the mod settings.

## Adding & removing

Safe to add to an existing save at any time. Removing it mid-save is also safe — your colony keeps playing — but any Contagion-specific infections currently in progress will simply vanish along with the mod.
